Privacy Policy

Diabetic Diet does not collect, store, or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no newsletter sign-ups, and no tracking scripts on these pages.

The calculators and tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Any numbers you enter stay on your device and are never sent to a server.

If you email us, we will use your address only to reply to your message. We do not share it with anyone else.

This policy may be updated if the site changes. The current version is always the one published on this page.

Diabetic Diet — educational information only, not medical advice. Always consult your doctor or diabetes care team about your personal diet.
